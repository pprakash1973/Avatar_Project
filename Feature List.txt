F01 — Travel & Booking Concierge

Priority: P1 — High

Provide a unified interface for travel research and booking assistance.

Functional requirements:

Search flights by origin, destination, dates, passenger count, and cabin class.

Compare fares, schedules, baggage allowances, and cancellation policies.

Search hotels by destination, dates, guest count, budget, location, and amenities.

Compare ratings, prices, availability, and booking conditions.

Support train and ground-transport research where integrations are available.

Generate itineraries and estimated trip budgets.

Provide booking links or integrate with authorized booking providers.

Track flight status and travel-related changes when supported.

Monitor prices when a suitable data source supports price tracking.

Send reminders for upcoming travel events.

Acceptance criteria:

Search results reflect the requested dates and constraints.

Prices and availability include their source and retrieval time where possible.

Estimated prices are clearly distinguished from confirmed prices.

Booking is completed only through a supported provider and with user approval.

Visual Understanding & Screen Assistance

Priority: P2 — Later Phase

Allow the assistant to analyze user-provided images, screenshots, or explicitly shared screen content.

Functional requirements:

Accept screenshots and image uploads.

Analyze visible interface elements, charts, and error messages.

Answer questions about supplied visual content.

Explain forms and provide step-by-step guidance.

Support live screen analysis only when the necessary capture capabilities and user permissions are available.

Avoid storing visual data without appropriate consent.

Example:
"Look at this error message and explain how I can fix it."

Acceptance criteria:

Responses are grounded in the supplied image or screen.

Unreadable content is identified as uncertain.

The assistant does not claim to see a screen or camera feed unless that capability is active.

F13 — Contextual & Emotion-Aware Conversation

Priority: P2 — Later Phase

Improve conversational naturalness and response adaptation.

Functional requirements:

Recognize conversational cues such as urgency, frustration, and uncertainty.

Adjust response length, tone, and pacing.

Support configurable assistant personalities.

Maintain context across follow-up questions.

Allow users to select professional, friendly, concise, or humorous styles.

Avoid claiming certainty about a user's emotions.

Acceptance criteria:

The assistant responds appropriately to explicit user preferences.

Personality settings do not compromise factual accuracy.

Users can change or disable conversational adaptations.
